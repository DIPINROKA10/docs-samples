# User guide: how to register for an event on FestNest

**Status:** draft — verify each step against the live UI before publishing (last checked 14 Oct 2026).

**Audience:** a college student using FestNest (https://festnest.in) for the first time.
**Goal:** find an event and complete registration in under 5 minutes.
**Time to complete:** ~4 minutes.

---

## Before you start

- A working email address you can open right now (for a verification mail).
- Your college name and roll number (some events ask for them).
- A phone with WhatsApp (optional but commonly requested for team communication).

You do **not** need an account to browse events.

---

## Step 1 — Open FestNest and pick your city

1. Go to https://festnest.in on a phone or laptop.
2. On the home page you'll see events grouped by type: hackathons, cultural fests,
   workshops, competitions, internships.
3. Use the city filter (top of the page) to set it to your city. Events near you sort
   to the top.

> **If nothing shows for your city:** clear the city filter and search by event name.

## Step 2 — Open an event card

1. Click an event card to open its detail page.
2. Check four things before registering:
   - **Date** — confirm it doesn't clash with exams or travel.
   - **Venue** — on-campus or online.
   - **Eligibility** — year, branch, solo or team.
   - **Deadline** — the registration closes on this date/time, not on the event date.

## Step 3 — Start registration

1. Click the **Register** button on the event page.
2. If this is your first time, FestNest will ask for:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Phone number
   - College name
3. Enter the details and click **Continue**.

## Step 4 — Verify your email

1. Check your inbox for a verification email from FestNest.
2. Click the verification link. (If you don't see it within 2 minutes, check the spam folder.)
3. You'll land back on FestNest with a "verified" confirmation.

## Step 5 — Complete team details (team events only)

1. For team events, you'll be asked to add teammates by email.
2. Enter each teammate's email address. FestNest sends them an invite.
3. You can add or remove teammates until the registration deadline.

> Each teammate must accept their invite before the team is considered complete.

## Step 6 — Confirm your registration

1. Review the summary page (event, team size, your details).
2. Click **Confirm registration**.
3. You'll see a success screen with a registration ID. **Save or screenshot it.**

## After you register

- You'll receive a confirmation email with the event's logistics (venue, contact
  person, reporting time) closer to the event date.
- To view or cancel a registration, open your profile on FestNest and go to "My
  registrations".

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Didn't get the verification email | Check spam; wait 2 minutes; click "resend" on the verification screen. |
| "Registration closed" | The deadline passed — registration is not reopened. |
| Wrong college selected | Open your profile and edit your college name, then re-register. |
| Teammate didn't get invite | Ask them to check spam; re-send from the team page. |
| Page won't load | Try a different browser or switch to mobile data. |

## Notes for the FestNest team (internal)

- The flow above assumes **email verification before confirmation**. If the live UI
  verifies only at the end, reorder steps 4 and 5 accordingly.
- Registration deadlines are quoted with a timezone; confirm it is IST before launch of any
  marketing copy.
